# Kiribati Translation Sheet — Nima-am-Bwatin

Thank you for translating! For each entry below, write the Kiribati (Gilbertese)
translation on the blank line under the English text. Keep any text inside
curly braces exactly as-is (e.g. `{name}`, `{dosage}`) — those get replaced
with real values by the app (a medication name, a dosage amount, etc.) and
must stay in your translation, in whatever position makes sense in Kiribati.

When you're done, send this file back and it will be transcribed into the app.

## Dietary advice (shown after a blood sugar reading is entered)

>

**dietary_advice.fbs_prediabetes**

Your fasting blood sugar is in the diabetes range. Please see a doctor or nurse soon. In the meantime, avoid sugary drinks and snacks, favor vegetables, fish, and small portions of rice, and stay active.
